XPO reported preliminary May 2026 freight metrics showing North American LTL tonnage per day up 0.5% year-over-year, driven by a 3.3% rise in daily shipments offset by a 2.7% decline in weight per shipment. The company noted actual results may differ from these preliminary figures.
