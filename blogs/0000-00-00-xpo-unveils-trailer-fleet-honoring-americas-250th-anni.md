XPO unveiled a new fleet of patriotic trailers, built at its Searcy, Arkansas manufacturing facility, featuring American flag-inspired branding ahead of the nation's 250th anniversary. The trailers will be operated by XPO drivers, including military veterans, with CEO Mario Harik noting that "the trucking industry is the backbone of the American economy."
