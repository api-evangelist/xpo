# XPO (xpo)

XPO, Inc. (NYSE: XPO) is a Fortune 500 transportation company and one of the largest providers of less-than-truckload (LTL) freight transportation in North America. Following the spin-offs of GXO Logistics (contract logistics, 2021) and RXO (brokerage, 2022), XPO is now a pure-play LTL trucking company operating a network of service centers across the United States and Mexico. XPO does not publish a public developer API or developer portal; customer integrations (rate quotes, shipment tracking, EDI) are arranged through enterprise account managers and standard EDI exchanges.
